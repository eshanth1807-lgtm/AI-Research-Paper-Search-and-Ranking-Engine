# Third-party components and research content

The MIT license in this repository applies to the original PaperLens code and documentation. It does not relicense third-party software, fonts, research papers, abstracts, or source metadata.

- **Cloudflare Tunnel:** cloudflared is a separate third-party program distributed by Cloudflare under its own license. Its executable is intentionally excluded from this repository. Obtain it from https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared and follow the license and notices there.
- **Research sources:** Live metadata is retrieved from Crossref and arXiv. Follow each source's applicable terms and the rights attached to individual papers and abstracts. Linked articles are not bundled with this application.
- **Demo data:** The six demo records link to real research papers. Their short synopses are labeled paraphrases, not full papers or publisher abstracts.
- **Fonts:** The stylesheet optionally loads DM Sans and Manrope through Google Fonts. Fonts are distributed under their respective upstream licenses and are not bundled here. System fonts remain available when Google Fonts cannot load.
- **Optional OpenAI API:** API access requires the user's own credentials and is subject to the provider's terms. No credentials are included.
